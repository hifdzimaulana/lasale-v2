Section II · The Institute

A Scholarly Foundation for the Practicing Bar

For thirty-seven years, LaSALe has served as the connective tissue between legal education and the practicing bar. Our curriculum is co-authored by sitting judges, tenured faculty, and senior partners — ensuring every program translates doctrine into craft.

Members access a continuous cycle of workshops, amicus participation, and supervised casework — culminating in our annual Certificate of Advanced Practice, recognized by 312 partner firms and 47 jurisdictions.
